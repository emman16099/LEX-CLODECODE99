# AI Marketer / AI Content-Strategist Agents (2024-2026), verified list as of 2026-11-26

Method note: product pages were read on 2026-11-25/26 through a page-fetch service (direct WebFetch to vendor domains was blocked in this sandbox). Most capability claims come from the vendors themselves, so treat them as marketing claims unless an independent source is cited. Every URL below was returned by a search or fetch during this session.

## Q1. Which named products are real, what do they do, and how do they compare? (master list)

### Takeaway
About 17 of the names are real, live products that act like a "content strategist" agent: they take a website or brand profile, plan a calendar, draft posts, publish with approval, and (in many cases) say they learn from results. The closest fits to "an AI that decides what to post every day and learns from analytics" are Holo, Emelyn, Naise AI, NoimosAI Social Agent, Blaze, Enrich Labs, Vendasta Social AI and Okara. Icon is defunct or pivoted, Narrato was acquired by Typeface, and several requested names (ContentBot, Cassidy, Copy.ai, Arvow, Ayay) are not social content-strategist agents.

### Inferences
- In 2025-2026 the category converged on one pattern: website URL → brand profile → strategy and calendar → drafts → approve → publish → results feed back. Holo, Blaze, Okara, Emelyn, Marky and Naise all start from "paste your website".
- Almost every product keeps a human approval step (HubSpot, Okara, Emelyn, Postiz, Blaze). "Full autopilot" is optional, not the default.
- Pricing has split into about $29-$99/mo SMB tools (Emelyn, Postiz, Marky, Enrich Labs, Blaze, Averi Solo, NoimosAI Pro), about $129-$499/mo "AI CMO" suites (Okara, NoimosAI, Blaze tier 2), and bundled or enterprise pricing (HubSpot, Vendasta, Typeface, Jasper, Writer, Nas.com).
- Icon's collapse (Mar 2026) and Blaze dropping its Autopilot page suggest "fully autonomous AI CMO" positioning has met market pushback. Blaze's new tagline is "You make the calls. Blaze does the work."

- Whether Blaze Autopilot still exists as a product name (the page returns 410).
- Friz's acquisition status; whether Naise has rebranded to "Emplifive".
- Funding for Enrich Labs, Emelyn, Naise and Okara (amounts). Okara is in Peak XV's portfolio, but I saw no amount.

## Q3. Which products use the user's email or CRM data as an input?

### Takeaway
None of the SMB social-strategist agents I verified read the user's email inbox. CRM data as an input is verified only for platform-native or enterprise products: HubSpot (CRM-native), Nas.com agents (explicit CRM), Typeface (owned audience data, Salesforce and Braze), Writer (customer knowledge) and Cassidy (CRM context, but not a social tool). Several SMB tools produce emails (Holo, Blaze, Ocoya, Enrich Labs) without reading them.

### Cited Findings

## Q4. Which products claim a closed feedback loop (analytics → next recommendations)?

### Takeaway
Explicit closed-loop claims: Holo, Emelyn, Naise AI, NoimosAI, Enrich Labs, Blaze, HubSpot Breeze (past performance as input) and Typeface (performance comes back as learnings). Partial or user-driven loops: Vendasta Social AI, Averi, Okara (SEO and AI-search audits) and Postiz (analytics plus rules). No loop claimed: Marky, Ocoya, Sintra Soshie, VibeFlow (not stated).

### Cited Findings
- Holo: "Gets smarter with every post… uses it to make the next week better"; "moves ad budget based on results" — [tryholo.ai](https://tryholo.ai/); independently restated — [Tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2026/10/15/vilnius-based-holo-raises-eur5m-to-bring-ai-powered-marketing-to-small-businesses/)
