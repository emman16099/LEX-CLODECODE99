# Established SaaS social media and AI writing tools that suggest what to post (as of 2026-11-26)

Method note: Pages were read with Perplexity Context, because direct WebFetch was blocked in this sandbox. Every page was fetched or cached between 2026-11-24 and 2026-11-26, and the page metadata dates are given where useful. Most claims come from vendor pages, so they describe what the vendor says the product does, not independently tested behaviour. Pricing is the vendor's published list price unless marked otherwise.

## Q1. Which products have AI "what to post" / idea / trend features as of late 2026? (master list, 22 products plus 2 extras)

### Takeaway
Every vendor on the brief now ships some AI ideation, but they work at very different depths. Most are prompt-driven caption or idea generators: Buffer, Canva, Sprinklr, Ocoya, Copy.ai, Writesonic and Jasper. A smaller group proactively proposes topics from live trends, listening data or the account's own performance, which is the "personal content producer" pattern. That group is Hootsuite Wisdom, Sprout Trellis, HubSpot's social and content agents, Later (Ideas plus Future Insights), Supergrow, Lately, Publer and Hypefury.

## Q2. Which ones read past post analytics to recommend future content (closed feedback loop)?

### Takeaway
A true closed loop, where the account's own post results feed into future suggestions, is documented for Lately, Supergrow, Publer, HubSpot, Hootsuite Wisdom/OwlyGPT, Hypefury, ContentIn and Sprout (Generate Posts). Taplio, FeedHive, Metricool and Later have partial loops: repurposing top posts, predicting scores, learning style from past posts, or using category-level trends. Buffer, Canva, Sprinklr AI+, Jasper, Copy.ai, Writesonic, Typefully and Planable did not document performance-based learning.

### Cited Findings
- **Lately**: the voice model is built from social analytics ("high-performing patterns of words, ideas, grammar") and refined by thumbs up, thumbs down and edits — [MarketingAI Space](https://marketingaispace.com/tools/lately); [Kompozy review](https://kompozy.io/reviews/lately-ai)
- **Supergrow**: post-by-post scoring against your average plus a "What to post next" content opportunity — [Supergrow](https://www.supergrow.ai/individuals)
- **Publer**: Brand Voice ingests the last 30 days of analytics and "constantly updates" — [Publer blog](https://publer.com/blog/publer-ai-with-brand-voices-and-analytics/)
- **HubSpot**: topic recommendations use "top-performing social posts" — [HubSpot KB](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/social/create-social-posts-with-content-agent). The Social Media Agent uses "past performance" — [HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com/products/marketing/social-media-ai-agent)
- **Hootsuite Wisdom**: analyses "performance metrics" and gives "tailored recommendations based on your goals and social data" — [Wisdom](https://www.hootsuite.com/platform/owly-writer-ai). OwlyGPT reviewed "historical data… without the need for manual data uploads" — [Hootsuite](https://hootsuite.com/whats-new/owly-gpt-hub-strategy)
- **Hypefury**: "learns from your best posts, your edits and what you reject" — [Hypefury](https://hypefury.com/llm-info)
- **ContentIn**: analytics "feed that data back into your voice training" — [ContentIn](https://contentin.io/feature/ai-ghostwriter/)
- **Sprout**: "Generate Posts by AI Assist" processes "Subscriber's published social messages with highest engagement" — [Sprout support](https://support.sproutsocial.com/hc/en-us/articles/20105027830669)
- **Partial loops:**
  - Taplio repurposes "top performing content straight from the analytics tab" — [Taplio](https://taplio.com/pricing)
  - FeedHive predicts scores before publishing and suggests recycling historical posts — [FeedHive AI](https://www.feedhive.com/features/ai); [FeedHive docs](https://docs.feedhive.com/recycling-content)
  - Metricool learns style from past posts (not results) — [Metricool Help](https://help.metricool.com/how-to-set-up-ai-prompt-customization-in-metricool-bl6y7)
  - Later's Ideas tab uses "what's performing well in your category" (category, not your account) and Caption Writer learns from thumbs up/down — [Later blog](https://later.com/blog/best-ai-social-media-tools-2026/); [Later blog](https://www.later.com/blog/caption-writer-for-instagram/)
  - Predis "reports back on what earned the click" (ads) — [Predis](https://predis.ai/pricing/)
- **Style-only learning, not performance:** Typefully learns from "writing samples and drafts" — [Typefully](https://typefully.com/content-ideas). Planable learns from "previous posts (to better match your existing content style)" — [Planable](https://planable.io/blog/planable-ai/)

### Inferences
- Lately is the longest-standing and most explicit "learns from engagement" product. Publer, Supergrow and HubSpot made the loop explicit in 2025–2026.
- Several "learns from your posts" claims, such as Typefully, Planable and Metricool, are about voice imitation, not results. A report should keep these two kinds of learning separate.

### Gaps
- No vendor publishes how strongly performance data weights the suggestions, or whether learning is continuous or a one-off ingestion. Publer says "constantly updates"; the others are vague.

Brand voice is now standard. Fully uploadable knowledge bases (documents or synced drives) are confirmed for Jasper, Copy.ai (Infobase), Writesonic, Publer (up to 5 files) and Hootsuite OwlyGPT/Wisdom (PDF/DOCX). Many SMB tools take brand voice only as text instructions, a website scan or sample posts.

## Q4. Which ones (if any) read the user's email inbox as an input?

### Takeaway
None of the established products I checked reads the user's email inbox to generate post ideas. The closest shipped feature is Supergrow's Content Radar, which mines meetings and Slack.
