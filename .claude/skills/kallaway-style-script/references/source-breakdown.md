## Repeated moves
- **Named terms:** triple hook, context, lean, context lean, contrarian snapback, god tier formula.
- **Escalation:** basic → most people mess up → 1% know how.
- **Rules with "must":** "must establish context", "must lean in".
- **Lists of three/four:** audio / visuals / text; proof / pain / benefit / shocking.
- **Turn-starters:** "Alright", "Now", "So".
- **Self-check question** the viewer can apply to their own work.
- **One analogy** at the hardest idea (fishing line).
- **Callback:** borrows the language everyone knows ("the curiosity loop you hear everybody talk about") and attaches his own name to it.

## Unproven claims in the source (do not copy)
- "scientifically impossible to skip", "physically cannot scroll away"
- "We have data…" — chart shown with no numbers or source
- "Like 1% of people know how to do this"
- "works every single time"
- "700,000 people have watched this" — a view count, not proof the method works

## Gap in the source
Steps 1 and 2 get a yes/no check. Step 3, called the most important, gets no test. When writing in this style, give every step a check.
