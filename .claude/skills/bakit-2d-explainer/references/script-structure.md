# Script structure for a 4–8 minute "bakit" video

Read at Step 2. The structure exists to keep viewers past the two places they leave: the first 30 seconds, and any stretch where nothing new is promised.

## Length and language

- 150 Tagalog words per minute of voice. 4 min ≈ 600 words, 5 min ≈ 750, 8 min ≈ 1,200.
- Tagalog with natural Taglish for business words (budget, ads, profit). Say numbers the way people say them: "isang milyon," not "1,000,000."
- Grade-5 words. One idea per sentence. Read it aloud in your head; if you run out of breath, split it.
- Clean voiceover only: no headings, no scene notes, no "(pause)". Scene notes live in the shot plan.
- One narrator voice (Manny's clone). Lia and Robo act on screen; the narrator tells us what they say ("Tanong ni Lia: bakit?"). No `LIA:` / `ROBO:` speaker labels, because HeyGen reads the script as one voice and a label would be spoken aloud.

## The 8 beats

| Beat | Share | Job | Write it like |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Hook | 0:00–0:05 | Contradiction, spoken | One hook formula from the playbook |
| 2. Promise | 0:05–0:15 | What they'll know by the end, and why it matters to their wallet | "Sa dulo nito, alam mo na kung..." |
| 3. Setup | ~10% | Show the familiar thing through Lia's day | Lia at the sari-sari, the mall, her phone |
| 4. Twist | ~5% | The fact that doesn't fit, with its source | "Pero ayon sa DTI..." |
| 5. Three reasons | ~50% | Reason 1, 2, 3, each ending in a mini-payoff and a bridge question | "Kaya pala... Pero bakit hindi lang nila...?" |
| 6. Gamit sa buhay | ~15% | What a small business owner or buyer does differently tomorrow | Concrete, 2–3 actions |
| 7. Balik sa tanong | ~5% | Answer the title question in one sentence | "So bakit...? Dahil..." |
| 8. CTA | ~5% | One ask, one open loop | "Sa susunod: bakit..." + subscribe |

### Inside each of the three reasons
1. **Pangalan.** A short sticky label Robo can hold up on a sign. ("Anchor Price")
2. **Kwento.** Lia experiences it in one scene.
3. **Bakit gumagana.** The mechanism, one or two sentences, with the source if it's a number.
4. **Mini-payoff.** A line that makes the viewer feel smart. ("Kaya pala parang nakatipid ka, pero hindi.")
5. **Tulay.** A question that only the next reason answers. This is the open loop that holds retention.

## Retention rules baked into the words

- A new noun or a new number at least every 10 seconds of speech. The shot planner flags silence of change in pictures; this rule flags it in words.
- Promise before you explain. "May tatlong dahilan" tells the brain to wait for three.
- Put the biggest surprise at Reason 2 or 3, not 1, so the middle holds.
- Callback once: bring the Hook image back at Beat 7 with the answer written on it.
- End with an open loop to the next video, not a long goodbye.

## Honesty rules

- Every number names its source in the script or description (DTI, PSA, BSP, a company's own report, a named news article).
- Lia's shop is fictional. If a beat uses a "client" example, say "halimbawa" and keep numbers round.
- If a reason is a theory, say "isa sa mga paliwanag" rather than "ang tunay na dahilan."

## Worked example (hook through Reason 1, ~120 words)

> Isang maliit na puno. Presyo: isang milyon. Bakit?
> Sa dulo nito, alam mo na kung bakit may bagay na mahal hindi dahil malaki, kundi dahil matagal. At makikita mo kung paano ito ginagamit ng mga negosyo sa'yo araw-araw.
> Si Lia, nakita niya sa plant show ang bonsai na kasing laki ng kanyang kamay. Tag-price: ₱980,000. Tumawa siya. Tapos tumingin siya sa label: "Edad: 86 taon."
> Unang dahilan: oras. Hindi mo mabibili ang 86 taon. Kaya ang binabayaran mo, hindi ang puno, kundi ang panahon na inalagaan ito.
> Kaya pala kahit maliit, mahal.
> Pero teka. May bonsai na 5 taon lang, ₱50,000 na. Hindi oras ang sagot doon. Ano?

Notice: contradiction at second 2, promise with a money angle, Lia experiences it, source-able number, mini-payoff, bridge question.
