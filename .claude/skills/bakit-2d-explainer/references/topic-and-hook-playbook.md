# Topic and hook playbook

How to choose what the video is about, how to open it, and how to title it. Read this at Step 1 and Step 6.

## Why topic matters more than art

On EhMagkano?, the closest Tagalog "bakit" channel, the median video gets about 50K views. Its breakouts (430K–1.1M) are not better drawn. They are better questions. Every one of them is a thing Filipinos already know, with a fact that doesn't fit:

| Breakout | Familiar thing | Contradiction |
|---|---|---|
| Bakit sobrang mahal ng Bonsai? (846K) | A tiny plant | Costs more than a car |
| Bakit hindi binabaha ang BGC? (549K) | Metro Manila floods | This part never does |
| Bakit nawala ang Ministop? (431K) | It was on every corner | It vanished |
| Ganito pala nagsimula ang Yakult (1.1M) | A drink every kid knows | A strange origin |
| Pink Himalayan Salt: marketing lang pala? (780K) | A premium product | The premium is a story |

Copy the shape, not the topics.

## The 6 topic shapes that work

1. **Maliit pero mahal / malaki pero mura.** A price that doesn't match the size. "Bakit ₱200 ang isang tasa ng kape sa mall?"
2. **Nandito, pero hindi.** Something that should happen here but doesn't, or vice versa. "Bakit walang Starbucks sa ilang probinsya?"
3. **Dati meron, ngayon wala.** A brand, habit or thing that disappeared. "Bakit nawala ang mga video rental?"
4. **Lahat gumagawa, konti lang kumikita.** A common hustle with a hidden catch. "Bakit lugi ang karamihan ng milk tea shop pagkatapos ng 1 taon?"
5. **Ganito pala.** A hidden mechanism behind a daily thing. "Paano kumikita ang GCash kung libre ito?"
6. **Ang tunay na dahilan.** A popular belief vs the documented reason. Only when you have a source; never invent a secret.

## Scoring card (0–5 each, ship at 18+)

| Axis | 5 means | 0 means |
|---|---|---|
| **Pamilyar** | Every Filipino viewer has touched this thing | Niche or foreign |
| **Kontradiksyon** | The title alone makes you say "teka, bakit nga?" | Just a fact |
| **Hanap** | People already search or argue about it (check YouTube/Google suggestions) | Nobody asks |
| **Funnel** | About money, negosyo, ads, OFW life, or a business lesson Manny can extend | Pure trivia |
| **Drawable** | Can be shown with Lia, Robo and 2–3 props per shot | Needs real footage or faces |

Write the score card out when you propose a topic. Example:

> **Bakit laging "sale" sa Shopee pero parang hindi bumababa ang presyo?**
> Pamilyar 5 · Kontradiksyon 5 · Hanap 4 · Funnel 5 (pricing psychology → ads lesson) · Drawable 4 = **23/25. Gawin.**

## Starter topic bank (score before use)

Pera at presyo: Bakit mahal ang sibuyas minsan? · Bakit ₱1 lang ang kita ng tindahan sa isang softdrink? · Bakit mas mura sa Divisoria? · Bakit may "₱99 lang" pero may dagdag na shipping?

Negosyo: Bakit nalulugi ang mga franchise ng food cart? · Bakit may negosyo na puno ng tao pero lugi? · Paano kumikita ang 7-Eleven kung maliit lang ang tubo bawat item? · Bakit nagsara ang maraming milk tea shop?

Ads at marketing (Manny's lane): Bakit lumalabas sa feed mo ang produktong kakausap mo lang? · Paano nakakaalam ang Facebook kung ano ang bibilhin mo? · Bakit ang ibang ads ₱5 lang kada click, ang iba ₱50? · Bakit "boost post" ang pinakamahal na paraan mag-ads?

OFW at pera abroad: Bakit mas malaki ang remittance fee sa ilang bansa? · Paano kumikita ang mga padala app? · Bakit mahal ang balikbayan box?

Araw-araw na Pinoy: Bakit may 13th month pay sa Pilipinas? · Bakit laging traffic sa EDSA kahit may MRT? · Bakit mas mahal ang gasolina sa probinsya?

## Hook formulas (first 5 seconds, spoken)

Pick one. All plant the contradiction before the viewer can scroll.

1. **Dalawang katotohanan na hindi tugma.** "Isang maliit na puno. Presyo: isang milyon. Bakit?"
2. **Ikaw na mismo.** "Nag-add to cart ka ba kagabi dahil 'sale'? Tingnan natin kung nakatipid ka talaga."
3. **Numero muna.** "Limampung porsyento ng milk tea shop, sarado bago mag-isang taon. Hindi dahil sa lasa."
4. **Tanong ni Lia.** Lia asks the exact question a viewer would, Robo says "Hindi mo gugustuhing malaman." Cut to title.
5. **Pangako ng gamit.** "Sa dulo nito, alam mo na kung paano hindi maloko sa susunod na 11.11."

Banned: "Hindi X ang problema, Y pala", "Akala mo X? Hindi.", fake urgency, questions you never answer, and any hook that promises a secret the script does not deliver.

## Title formulas (Step 6, write 3)

- `Bakit [pamilyar na bagay] [kontradiksyon]?` — "Bakit sobrang mahal ng kape sa mall?"
- `[Bagay]: [kontradiksyon], [dahilan sa 2 salita]?` — "Shopee Sale: Mas Mahal Pala? Ganito ang Trick."
- `Paano [kumikita/nalulugi] ang [bagay] kung [kontradiksyon]?` — "Paano kumikita ang GCash kung libre ito?"

Rules: Tagalog or Taglish, under 60 characters, one capitalized key word allowed, no clickbait the script can't pay off. Flag any title that could read as a real client result.

## Thumbnail text (2–4 words)

The thumbnail says the contradiction, the title asks the question. Bonsai video: thumbnail "₱1M?!" over a tiny tree; title asks why. Never repeat the title on the thumbnail. Lia's face shows the emotion of the twist (shock, suspicion, lightbulb). Keep bottom-right clear for the timestamp. Test readability at 320 px wide.

## Shorts cutdowns

Two per video. (a) Hook + first payoff, 30–45 s, ends "Buong sagot sa channel." (b) The single most surprising beat, 20–30 s, opens mid-sentence on the number. Shorts bring reach but convert poorly to subscribers, so the long video is the product and the Shorts are the posters.
