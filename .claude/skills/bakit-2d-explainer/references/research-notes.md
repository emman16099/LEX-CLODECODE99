# Research notes (Nov 2026)

The evidence behind the rules in this skill. Numbers come from third-party trackers unless marked official; re-check before quoting them in a video.

## Tagalog "bakit" explainers today

- **EhMagkano?** — 96K subs, 31.5M views, 633 videos, 98% long-form, stock footage + voiceover. Median ~50K views; breakouts 430K–1.1M all follow "familiar thing + contradiction" (Bonsai 846K, BGC floods 549K, Ministop 431K, Yakult 1.1M). Audience 25–34, PH + OFW. https://outlierkit.com/channel/ehmagkanoyt
- **Kaalaman** — 2.9M subs but +10K subs in the last year; stalled. https://asknaveen.com/channel/@kaalamanhq
- **The Taglish Explainer** — new (Dec 2025), talking-head. Early-stage competitor.
- No Tagalog channel is doing "bakit" explainers in 2D cartoon at scale. The lane is open and unproven.

## The style model: Kok Bisa? (Indonesia)

6.1M subs (Oct 2025). Bright saturated 2D, local humor, recurring cast, videos under 5 min, topics crowdsourced from viewer questions. Kumalasari & Soko (2026), comparing it with Kurzgesagt, found both effective, with Kok Bisa winning through "cultural proximity," everyday language and high-contrast color. https://eu-opensci.org/index.php/ejedu/article/view/70181/13903

## Global styles, briefly

- Kurzgesagt 25.6M: flat vector, dark navy, weeks per video; heavily imitated. https://videocue.io/looks/kurzgesagt-flat-vector-icon
- The Infographics Show 15.5M, 6,323 videos: template flat style at daily volume.
- Sam O'Nella 4.8M: crude MS-Paint; works only because the writing is funny. https://becomeviral.com/blog/sam-onella-academy-case-study
- Neuron Visuals: minimalist line animation, 1B+ Reels views; fits quotes and psychology, not how-to. https://neuronvisuals.com/

## Retention and format

- Animated Shorts finish well but convert poorly to subscribers vs talking head. https://fluxnote.io/guides/youtube-shorts-animation-format-2026
- Agency benchmarks: pattern interrupt every 8–10 s; under-5-min animated videos should aim for 60–70% average view; 5–10 min for 40–50%. https://longstories.ai/blog/retention-benchmarks-animated-youtube-videos
- AI "What if" 2D channels: IFOLOGY posts 15/week, 4.2M views and only 41K subs in under a year. Volume gets views, not loyalty. https://www.ytproanalytics.com/yt-stats/channel/UCv4DniMRgnBTmfFaxeMTTLA

## AI pipeline proof from the Philippines

FUNTASTIC YT (Filipino student): ~600K subs, ~500M views, $9K in one month, ChatGPT + Kling, 1–2 hours per video. https://www.npr.org/2025/08/28/nx-s1-5493485/ai-slop-videos-youtube-tiktok

## Money

Philippines creator RPM ≈ $0.66 per 1,000 views (Dynamoi, Nov 2025–Nov 2026); 1M views ≈ $660. Finance/OFW niches and US/Gulf viewers pay 3–5×. https://dynamoi.com/data/youtube-adsense-rpm/ph — so the channel is a trust funnel for the ads service, not an ad-revenue business.

## YouTube policy (official)

Monetization policy renamed "repetitious" to "inauthentic content" in July 2025. Allowed: AI used "to visualize a unique character and narrative you invented." Not allowed: "AI-generated content made with generic or unoriginal templates, giving the impression of mass production." Enforcement is channel-level. https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392
