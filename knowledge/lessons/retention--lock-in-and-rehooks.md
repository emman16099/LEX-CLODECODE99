# Plan the script in blocks: hook → lock-in → body → re-hook → body → CTA

## The pattern
1. **Hook** (1 line).
2. **Lock-in** (2–3 sentences right after the hook that confirm "yes, this is worth your time").
3. **Body block 1** → **re-hook** (a new mini-hook to stop people leaving) → **body block 2** → re-hook → optional block 3.
4. **CTA** that matches the topic (a free guide on the same thing).

## Why it works
People decide to leave in waves, not only at the start. A re-hook gives them a
new reason to stay right where they would drift away.

## Example (our niche, written by us)
Hook: "This cabinet mistake costs $1,800." → Lock-in: "I see it in 1 of 3 kitchens. Here's how to spot it in 10 seconds." → Body → Re-hook: "But the worst part is the hinge…"
