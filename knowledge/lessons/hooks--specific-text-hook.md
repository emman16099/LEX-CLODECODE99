# The on-screen text hook should name a pain, a dream result, or a change, with a specific number

## The pattern
- Text hook = a subject + one of: a **pain** reminder, a **dream result** teaser, or a **before→after change**.
- Use exact numbers ("22 days", "$2,700"), not fuzzy ones ("a few weeks").
- Cut filler words (and, but, therefore). Max 2 lines. Break lines where the phrase naturally breaks.
- Put it about one-third down the screen, not over the face.

## Why it works
A scrolling thumb reads the text before it hears anything. A specific number
feels real and checkable, so the brain stops to see if it's true.

## Example (our niche, written by us)
"Kitchen redo in 9 days (not 9 weeks)"
