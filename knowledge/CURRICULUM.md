# Curriculum — what the attention agent must learn

Study in this order. Each topic maps to a step in `PURPOSE.md`.
Tick a question when it has at least one level-B lesson in `RULEBOOK.md`.

## 1. Audience (before anything else)
- [ ] Who is the buyer, and what do they worry about or want most?
- [ ] What words do they use? (comments, reviews, search terms)

## 2. Hooks — STOP (first 1–3 seconds)
- [ ] Spoken hooks: which hook types win in our niche? (question, mistake, contrarian, result-first, story)
- [ ] Text hooks: what on-screen text works in the first frame?
- [ ] Visual hooks: first-frame movement, surprise, before/after.

## 3. Retention — HOLD
- [ ] Script structure: hook → "lock-in" lines → body → re-hooks → close.
- [ ] Pacing and editing: cuts, captions, b-roll, length.
- [ ] Open loops and story: how to make people wait for the answer.

## 4. Payoff — PAY BACK
- [ ] How to deliver the promised value fast and clearly.
- [ ] Proof: before/after, numbers, demos, testimonials.

## 5. Call to action — MOVE
- [ ] One clear next step: comment-keyword, link, DM, call, form.
- [ ] Offer: what makes the next step worth it?

## 6. Packaging
- [ ] Thumbnails and titles (YouTube).
- [ ] Captions, cover frames, first comment (TikTok/Reels).
- [ ] Ad creative rules for Facebook/Instagram ads.

## 7. Platform rules (these expire — recheck every 6 months)
- [ ] TikTok, Reels, Shorts, YouTube, Facebook: length, format, what each rewards.
- [ ] Ad policies (what gets ads rejected).

## 8. Reading results
- [ ] Which numbers to check for each step (hook rate, hold rate, CTR, cost per lead).
- [ ] How to run a fair test (change one thing at a time).
